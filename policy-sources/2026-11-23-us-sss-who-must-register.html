Who Needs to Register

Almost all male US citizens and male immigrants, who are 18 through 25, are required to register with Selective Service.

A chart of who must register is also available in PDF.

Any right, privilege, or benefit requiring Selective Service registration is determined by the agency or organization reviewing the request. The Selective Service System does not make eligibility determinations.
