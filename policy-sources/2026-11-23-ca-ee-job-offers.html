Job offer changes

As of November 3, 2026, we’ve changed how we assess job offers under Express Entry:

  • You don't need a Labour Market Impact Assessment (LMIA) for a job offer to qualify.
  • Having an LMIA won’t give you more Comprehensive Ranking System points or give your profile priority.
  • Job offers may be for an existing or a new position.
  • Job offers may be with an existing or a new employer.
  • Officers will still assess whether a job offer is genuine.

If you have a job offer that isn’t included in your profile, you should update it.

The system is recalculating existing profiles.

  • This process may take up to two weeks.
  • During this time, we’ll pause invitation rounds and will start again when the process is complete.
What hasn’t changed

Programs that required a valid job offer as part of their eligibility criteria still require one, including

  • the Federal Skilled Trades Program
  • the Federal Skilled Worker Program
  • some streams under provincial nominee programs

Valid job offers to meet eligibility criteria can continue to be in either a non-high-wage or high-wage occupation, but an LMIA will no longer be required for these job offers.

You should still include the details of any job offers in your Express Entry profile so that you stay eligible for these programs.

Job offer basics

If you have a new job offer (offer of employment), you need to update your Express Entry profile in your IRCC secure account with the

  • start date
  • employer name and address
  • National Occupational Classification (NOC) code related to the job

How to update your profile if you have a job offer without an LMIA

As of November 3, 2026, while our requirements have changed, our online system doesn’t yet reflect that change.

In your profile, you’ll still be required to select “yes” when asked if you have a positive LMIA, even though you don’t need one.

In your profile, under “Work history”, include that you have a job offer with the following details to ensure that your job offer can be qualified without an LMIA:

Question or field Response
Was a positive LMIA issued for the job offer in Canada? Yes
LMIA Number 00000
LMIA Issuance Date January 1, 2026
LMIA Expiry Date January 1, 2029

Your job offer must

  • be recent
  • be in writing
  • not be from an embassy, high commission or consulate in Canada
  • be for at least 1 year if you’re accepted as a permanent resident
  • set out the details of the job they’re offering you, such as:
    • your pay and deductions
    • your duties
    • the conditions of employment, like your hours of work

Your job offer must also meet other criteria to be valid under the Express Entry program you qualify for below.

If you’re a physician with a job offer in Canada

We introduced a temporary public policy for foreign national physicians who:

  • were invited to apply for permanent residence through Express Entry on or after April 25, 2023, and submitted an application
  • have a job offer to provide publicly funded medical services in Canada

Under the policy, your job offer:

  • is valid under the Federal Skilled Worker Program (FSWP) even if the job being offered is not continuous and does not last for at least 1 year after we issue your permanent resident visa
  • allows you to earn points:
    • for arranged employment under the FSWP selection factors
    • toward your total Express Entry score in the Comprehensive Ranking System (CRS)

Learn more about our updated requirements for a valid job offer to work as a physician.

If you’re a university professor or lecturer with a job offer in Canada

We introduced a temporary public policy for foreign nationals who:

  • were invited to apply for permanent residence through Express Entry on or after November 17, 2026, and submitted an application
  • have a job offer in NOC 41200 (University professors and lecturers) that will be paid by a Canadian entity through non-wage compensation (such as stipends, bursaries and scholarships).

Your job offer:

  • is now valid under the Federal Skilled Worker Program (FSWP) and the Canadian Experience Class (CEC) even if the job being offered is paid through non-wage compensation (such as stipends, bursaries and scholarships)
  • allows you to earn points:
    • for arranged employment under the FSWP selection factors
    • toward your total Express Entry score in the Comprehensive Ranking System (CRS)

Genuineness assessment

An officer will assess your job offer and must be satisfied that it’s genuine.

This includes whether

  • the employer
    • is active in the business
    • can fulfill the terms of their offer
    • made an offer that meets their needs
    • is following employment and recruitment laws
  • you are
    • qualified and able to perform the work
    • likely to accept the offer and carry out the job

Include any supporting documents with your job offer that supports its genuineness.

Other requirements by program

Federal Skilled Workers Program

If you don’t have a valid job offer, you’ll need to provide proof of funds to be approved. A valid job offer has to be

  • from 1 employer
  • continuous
  • paid
  • full-time (at least 30 hours a week)
  • not seasonal
  • for at least 1 year if you’re accepted as a permanent resident
  • in a NOC TEER category 0, 1, 2 or 3
  • genuine

Federal Skilled Trades Program

If you don’t have a valid job offer, you’ll need a certificate of qualification in your skilled trade. A valid job offer has to be:

  • made by up to 2 employers
  • for continuous, paid, full-time work (at least 30 hours a week)
  • for at least 1 year if you’re accepted as a permanent resident
  • for a job under one of these NOC 2021 TEER 2 and 3 categories:
    • Major Group 72, technical trades and transportation officers and controllers:
      • excluding Sub-major Group 726, transportation officers and controllers
    • Major Group 73, general trades
    • Major Group 82, supervisors in natural resources, agriculture and related production
    • Major Group 83, occupations in natural resources and related production
    • Major Group 92, processing, manufacturing and utilities supervisors, and utilities operators and controllers
    • Major Group 93, central control and process operators and aircraft assembly assemblers and inspectors, excluding Sub-major Group 932, aircraft assemblers and aircraft assembly inspectors
    • Minor Group 6320, cooks, butchers and bakers
    • Unit Group 62200, chefs
  • genuine

Canadian Experience Class

You don’t need a job offer to be eligible, but your employer may choose to give you one.

Provincial Nominee Program

You may need a valid job offer, depending on the Provincial Nominee Program you’re eligible for, to be considered.
