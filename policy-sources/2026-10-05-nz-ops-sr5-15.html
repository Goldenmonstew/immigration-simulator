SR5.15 Requirements for 24 months of work in New Zealand in a Tier 2 Green List role

  1. Applicants must have worked for at least 24 months in New Zealand in an occupation on Tier 2 of the Green List during the 30-month period immediately before the date the residence application is made.
  2. To be considered as having worked in a Tier 2 Green List occupation, applicants must have met the requirements for their occupation as specified in the Green List for the duration of the 24 month period.
  3. For the 24 months of work in New Zealand, applicants must have been working full-time (at least 30 hours a week) in a Tier 2 Green List occupation and held one of the following visas:
    1. any work visa; or
    2. a critical purpose visitor visa with work conditions; or
    3. an interim visa granted while the applicant held a work visa or a critical purpose visitor visa with work conditions and had applied for a further work visa.
  4. Where an occupation has remuneration requirements, the work period will start to be counted when the applicant’s remuneration reaches the hourly rate specified in Tier 2 of Appendix 13 for that occupation in effect at that time (provided they hold a visa described in (c) above).
  5. Where an occupation does not have remuneration requirements, the work period will start to be counted when the applicant’s remuneration is at or above the median wage (see SR5.15.1) in effect at that time (provided they hold a visa described in (c) above).
  6. Despite (d) and (e), where an applicant’s work visa was granted, and they began to accrue skilled work experience in New Zealand within five months of the grant date, the relevant wage rate in effect on the grant date will apply (see examples at SR5.5).
  7. The work period (while earning the specified hourly rate) will stop being counted if:
    1. the applicant ceases to hold one of the visas listed in (c) above; or
    2. the applicant changes occupation and their new income is below the required wage rate for that occupation, which is either:
      • the median wage (SR5.15.1); or
      • the hourly rate specified in Tier 2 of Appendix 13 for that occupation; or
    3. the applicant’s remuneration decreases, and the new rate is below the required rate for that occupation, which is either:
      • the median wage (SR5.15.1); or
      • the hourly rate specified in Tier 2 of Appendix 13 for that occupation.
  8. A table showing the median wage rate and the wage rates for Green List Tier 2 occupations (Appendix 13) with remuneration requirements in effect across time is set out under SR5.15.1.
  9. For the purposes of (g), the required wage rate is the relevant wage rate that applied for that occupation at the point in time specified at SR5.5(b)(iv).

    Note: Where remuneration requirements apply, work in New Zealand will not stop being counted if the specified hourly rate in immigration instructions increases as long as the applicant’s income does not decrease and they have not changed occupation.
    For example, a crane operator must be paid an hourly rate of 1.3 times the median wage. An applicant can start counting work experience from 18 August 2025 if they earn $43.63 an hour (1.3 times the median wage in effect at the time). When the wage requirement increases to $45.50 an hour on 9 March 2026, they can keep counting their work if they are still a crane operator earning $43.63 an hour.

  10. Despite (e) and (g) above, where an occupation does not have remuneration requirements, an applicant who who accrued skilled work experience in that occupation while holding a visa that they applied for before 29 September 2023 either:
    1. must meet the median wage rate in effect at the time when they began to accrue skilled work experience in New Zealand that counts towards their residence requirements (and have continued to meet that wage rate for their claimed work experience), or
    2. does not need to meet any wage requirement for the skilled work experience provided their current employment or offer of employment is at or above the median wage at the time they make their resident visa application.
  11. For the purposes of these instructions, any leave consistent with section 16 of the Holidays Act 2003 (including parental leave and ACC leave) taken during a period of work in New Zealand in a Tier 2 Green List role can be counted.
  12. Despite (a) above, where an occupation is removed from the Tier 2 Green List after the applicant has started to count work in New Zealand towards the 24 months required, the work can continue to be counted if:
    1. the applicant remains in the occupation; and
    2. continues to meet the requirements of the Green List as they were listed for the duration of the 24 month period.
  13. If an applicant has worked in New Zealand for at least 24 months in the last 30 month period but has not maintained a role in a Green List Tier 2 occupation for the full 24 months, they can still be considered for the grant of residence if they meet the requirements set out for work in New Zealand earning at least twice the median wage (see SR5.20) for the balance of the 24 months.

    Note: The 24 months of work in New Zealand does not have to be gained consecutively and can be a total of different periods, for example two 12-month periods with a gap of six months between. Additionally, it can be made up of different types of work in New Zealand, for example 18 months in a Tier 2 Green List role and six months in a role paid twice the median wage.
