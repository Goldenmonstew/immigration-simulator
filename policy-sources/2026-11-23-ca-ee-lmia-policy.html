Temporary public policy to exempt Labour Market Impact Assessments and related requirements for qualifying job offers for eligibility under Express Entry

Complete title: Temporary public policy to exempt Labour Market Impact Assessments and related requirements for qualifying job offers for eligibility under the Federal Skilled Worker Program and the Federal Skilled Trades Program

Background

Under Express Entry, job offers are part of the program eligibility for the Federal Skilled Worker (FSW) program and the Federal Skilled Trades (FST) program. Currently, job offer requirements vary by the foreign national's work permit type and status. In most cases, job offers must be supported by a Labour Market Impact Assessment (LMIA) administered by Employment and Social Development Canada (ESDC), unless they meet specific exemption requirements.

The LMIA requirement results in two separate assessments for Express Entry job offers: ESDC's LMIA for the proposed work and Immigration, Refugees and Citizenship Canada's (IRCC's) assessment of the foreign national and their ability to perform the work. Requiring both assessments creates barriers for many applicants and employers. Obtaining a positive LMIA can delay a candidate's chances for an invitation to apply in Express Entry as an LMIA must be acquired in advance as part of program eligibility to enter the pool. In addition, several LMIA components were designed for the temporary foreign worker context and may not be pertinent for permanent resident selection.

In many cases, qualifying job offers are based on a candidate's current employment in Canada. Nevertheless, unless a specific exemption applies, employers must obtain an LMIA even when the offer is based on ongoing employment, creating administrative burden. This may disincentivize employers from supporting their workers in the permanent residence application process.

On November 3, 2026, IRCC amended the Express Entry Ministerial Instructions to introduce points for job offers and Canadian work experience in high-wage occupations in the Comprehensive Ranking System (CRS) to refocus the ranking system on the strongest predictors of positive economic outcomes and contribute to the Talent Attraction Strategy. The new job offer requirements do not include any LMIA-related assessments to qualify. This change integrates the separate job offer assessment processes previously managed by IRCC and ESDC into a single process led by IRCC.

Public policy considerations

This public policy enhances consistency and efficiency in assessing qualifying job offers for eligibility under the FSW and the FST.

By exempting the LMIA requirement for qualifying job offers, this public policy intends to:

  • reduce the administrative burden for applicants and employers who would otherwise be required to obtain an LMIA;
  • establish a single review process by IRCC that fully assesses qualifying job offers, improving timeliness and efficiency;
  • focus job offer assessments on factors important for selecting permanent residents; and
  • support equitable assessments through a consistent assessment process and decision-making for all applicants, regardless of work permit type or status.

Therefore, I hereby establish, pursuant to my authority under section 25.2 of the Immigration and Refugee Protection Act (the Act), that there are sufficient public policy considerations that justify the granting of exemptions from the requirements of the Regulations listed below to foreign nationals who meet the conditions (eligibility requirements) set out below.

Conditions (eligibility requirements)

Based on public policy considerations, when processing an application for a permanent resident visa, delegated officers may grant an exemption from the requirements of the Regulations identified below to a foreign national who meets the following conditions:

Part 1: The foreign national

  1. has submitted an application under section 70 of the Regulations as a member of the Federal Skilled Worker Class as described in section 75 of the Regulations, following an Express Entry invitation to apply for a permanent resident visa, issued on or after November 3, 2026; and
  2. has been offered arranged employment, as defined in subsection 82(1) of the Regulations, and that offer:
    1. is made by an employer that is actively engaged in the business in respect of which the offer is made;
    2. is consistent with the reasonable employment needs of the employer;
    3. contains terms that the employer is reasonably able to fulfill;
    4. is for employment in an occupation that the foreign national has past work experience or education; and
    5. was made by an employer with no previous non-compliance with the federal or provincial laws that regulate the employment or recruitment of employees, including foreign nationals, in the province in which it is intended that the foreign national will work.

Part 2 - The foreign national

  1. has submitted an application under section 70 of the Regulations as a member of the Federal Skilled Trades Class as described in section 87.2 of the Regulations, following an Express Entry invitation to apply for a permanent resident visa, issued on or after November 3, 2026; and
  2. has been offered employment, and that offer
    1. is made by an employer that is actively engaged in the business in respect of which the offer is made;
    2. is consistent with the reasonable employment needs of the employer;
    3. contains terms that the employer is reasonably able to fulfill;
    4. is for employment in an occupation that the foreign national has past work experience or education; and
    5. was made by an employer with no previous non-compliance with the federal or provincial laws that regulate the employment or recruitment of employees, including foreign nationals, in the province in which it is intended that the foreign national will work.

Provisions of the Regulations for which an exemption may be granted to foreign nationals who meet the conditions in Part 1 above

  • The requirement in subparagraph 82(2)(a)(i) – for the skilled worker's work permit to have been issued based on a positive determination made by an officer under subsection 203(1);
  • Subparagraph 82(2)(a)(ii) – the requirement for the skilled worker to be working for an employer specified on the work permit;
  • Subparagraph 82(2)(a)(iii) – the requirement for the skilled worker's current employer to have offered employment;
  • Subparagraph 82(2)(b)(i) – the requirement that the skilled worker be working for an employer specified on the work permit;
  • Subparagraph 82(2)(b)(ii) – the requirement for the skilled worker to have been offered employment from the employer on their work permit;
  • Subparagraph 82(2)(b)(iii) – the requirement for the skilled worker to have accumulated at least one year of full-time work experience, or the equivalent in part-time work, over a continuous period of work for the employer on their work permit;
  • Subparagraph 82(2)(c)(ii) – the requirement for an officer to have approved the skilled worker's offer of employment based on a valid assessment – provided to the officer by the Department of Employment and Social Development – that the requirements set out in subsection 203(1) with respect to the offer have been met;
  • The requirement in subparagraph 82(2)(d)(iii) – for the skilled worker to have the circumstances described in subparagraph 82(2)(c)(ii);
  • The requirement in paragraph 83(1)(e) – for the skilled worker to have been awarded points for employment in Canada under subparagraphs 82(2)(a)(i), (ii), and (iii), 82(2)(b)(i), (ii), and (iii), or 82(2)(c)(ii).

Provisions of the Regulations for which an exemption may be granted to foreign nationals who meet the conditions in Part 2 above

  • Clause 87.2(3)(d)(ii)(A) – the requirement for a foreign national's work permit to have been issued based on a positive determination made by an officer under subsection 203(1) with respect to their employment with their current employer in a skilled trade occupation;
  • Clause 87.2(3)(d)(ii)(B) – the requirement for the foreign national to be working for the employer specified on their work permit;
  • The requirement in clause 87.2(3)(d)(ii)(D) – for the foreign national to have an offer of employment made by up to two employers that are specified on their work permit.
  • Clause 87.2(3)(d)(iii)(A) – the requirement for the foreign national to be working for the employer specified on their work permit;
  • The requirement in clause 87.2(3)(d)(iii)(C) – for the foreign national to have an offer of employment made by up to two employers that are specified on their work permit;
  • Clause 87.2(3)(d)(iii)(D) – the requirement for the foreign national to have accumulated at least one year of full-time work experience, or the equivalent in part-time work, over a continuous period of work for the employers who made the job offer;
  • Clause 87.2(3)(d)(iv)(C) – the requirement for an officer to have approved the foreign national's offer of employment based on a valid assessment – provided to the officer by the Department of Employment and Social Development – that the requirements set out in subsection 203(1) with respect to the offer have been met;
  • The requirement in clause 87.2(3)(d)(v)(C) – for the foreign national to have the circumstances described in clause 87.2(3)(d)(iv)(C).

Other admissibility and selection criteria

Foreign nationals eligible under this public policy are subject to all other legislative obligations and admissibility and selection criteria not exempted under this, or another, public policy.

Effective date and expiration

This public policy takes effect on November 3, 2026 and remains in effect until it is revoked by the Minister. This public policy may be revoked at any time.

The Hon. Lena Metlege Diab, ECNS, KC, P.C., M.P.
Minister of Citizenship and Immigration
Dated at Ottawa, this 15th day of October, 2026
