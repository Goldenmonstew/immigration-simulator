Ministerial Instructions Amending the Ministerial Instructions Respecting the Express Entry System, 2026-1

Department Of Citizenship And Immigration

Immigration And Refugee Protection Act

The Minister of Citizenship and Immigration gives the annexed Ministerial Instructions Amending the Ministerial Instructions Respecting the Express Entry System, 2026-1, under paragraph 10.3(1)(h)Footnote a of the Immigration and Refugee Protection ActFootnote b.

Ottawa, October 15, 2026
Lena Metlege Diab
Minister of Citizenship and Immigration

Amendments

1 (1) The definition qualifying offer of arranged employment in section 1 of the Ministerial Instructions Respecting the Express Entry SystemFootnote 1 is repealed.

(2) Section 1 of the Instructions is amended by adding the following in alphabetical order:

high-wage occupation means an occupation in respect of which the median wage is at least 1.3 times the median wage of the entire Canadian workforce. (profession à salaire élevé)

high-wage occupation level means, in respect of a high-wage occupation, one of the following levels:

  • (a) the level of 2 times the median wage, if the median wage for the occupation is at least 2 times the median wage of the entire Canadian workforce;
  • (b) the level of 1.5 times the median wage, if the median wage for the occupation is at least 1.5 times, but less than 2 times, the median wage of the entire Canadian workforce;
  • (c) the level of 1.3 times the median wage, if the median wage for the occupation is at least 1.3 times, but less than 1.5 times, the median wage of the entire Canadian workforce. (seuil applicable à une profession à salaire élevé)

list of high-wage occupations means a list prepared by the Department of Citizenship and Immigration and published on its website, as amended from time to time, that

  • (a) sets out the high-wage occupations according to the high-wage occupation level; and
  • (b) is based on data from the Job Bank, Labour Force Survey and Census of Population for the three years preceding the publication of the list. (liste des professions à salaire élevé)

qualifying offer of employment means a qualifying offer of employment within the meaning of subsection 15.2(2). (offre d’emploi admissible)

2 Paragraphs 7(1)(k) and (l) of the Instructions are amended by replacing “qualifying offer of arranged employment” with “qualifying offer of employment”.

3 (1) Subparagraph 8(1)(a)(iv) of the Instructions is replaced by the following:

  • (iv) labour market integration;

(2) The portion of subsection 8(2) of the Instructions before paragraph (a) is replaced by the following:

Comprehensive Ranking System — total number of points

(2) The total number of points that may be assigned under the Comprehensive Ranking System is 1270 points, allocated as follows:

(3) Subparagraphs 8(2)(a)(i) and (ii) of the Instructions are replaced by the following:

  • (i) a maximum of 570 points, if the foreign national has no accompanying spouse or common-law partner, or
  • (ii) a maximum of 530 points, if the foreign national has an accompanying spouse or common-law partner;

4 Section 15 of the Instructions is replaced by the following:

Labour market integration

15 (1) The points that are to be assigned for the labour market integration factor referred to in subparagraph 8(1)(a)(iv) are the greater of

  • (a) the points assigned under subsection 15.1(1) for Canadian work experience, and
  • (b) the points assigned under subsection 15.2(1) for a qualifying offer of employment.
Maximum points

(2) The maximum number of points that may be assigned for the labour market integration factor is

  • (a) 150 points, if the foreign national has no accompanying spouse or common-law partner; or
  • (b) 140 points, if the foreign national has an accompanying spouse or common-law partner.

Canadian work experience

15.1 (1) The points that are to be assigned for Canadian work experience are the sum of

  • (a) the points assigned under subsection (7) for Canadian work experience by duration, and
  • (b) the points assigned under subsection (8) for Canadian work experience in a high-wage occupation.
Canadian work experience — rules of application

(2) For the purposes of this section, Canadian work experience is full-time work experience, or the full-time equivalent for part-time work experience, that is acquired by a foreign national in Canada within the 10-year period preceding the day on which points are assigned under subsection (7) or (8) in one or more occupations listed in

  • (a) unit group 31100, 31101 or 31102 of the National Occupational Classification, if, during that experience, the foreign national provides publicly funded medical services; or
  • (b) TEER Category 0, 1, 2 or 3 of the National Occupational Classification, if the foreign national acquires the experience with one or more employers and is remunerated by the payment of wages or a commission.
Requirements

(3) In order to be assigned points under subsection (7) or (8) for their Canadian work experience, a foreign national

  • (a) must specify in their expression of interest the five-digit code in the National Occupational Classification that corresponds to each of the occupations they have engaged in while accumulating their Canadian work experience; and
  • (b) must have performed the actions described in the lead statement for the occupation as set out in the occupational descriptions of the National Occupational Classification, as well as a substantial number of the main duties, including all essential duties, as set out in the National Occupational Classification.
Full-time work

(4) For the purposes of this section, full-time work consists of at least 30 hours of work per week.

Work in excess

(5) For the purposes of this section, a period of work experience that exceeds full-time work in one occupation or simultaneous periods of work experience in more than one full-time occupation are to be evaluated as a single period of full-time work experience in a single occupation.

Work experience — requirements

(6) For the purposes of this section,

  • (a) a period of employment during which the foreign national was engaged in full-time study is not to be included in calculating a period of work experience;
  • (b) a period of self-employment is not to be included in calculating a period of work experience referred to in paragraph (2)(b);
  • (c) a period of unauthorized work is not to be included in calculating a period of work experience;
  • (d) the foreign national must have had temporary resident status during their period of work experience and any period of full-time study or training; and
  • (e) the full-time equivalent for part-time work experience is 30 hours of work per week.
Points for experience by duration

(7) The points that are to be assigned for Canadian work experience by duration are the following:

  • (a) for a foreign national who has no accompanying spouse or common-law partner
    • (i) 0 points, for no Canadian work experience or less than one year of such experience,
    • (ii) 40 points, for one year of Canadian work experience,
    • (iii) 53 points, for two years of Canadian work experience,
    • (iv) 64 points, for three years of Canadian work experience,
    • (v) 72 points, for four years of Canadian work experience, and
    • (vi) 80 points, for five or more years of Canadian work experience; and
  • (b) for a foreign national who has an accompanying spouse or common-law partner
    • (i) 0 points, for no Canadian work experience or less than one year of such experience,
    • (ii) 35 points, for one year of Canadian work experience,
    • (iii) 46 points, for two years of Canadian work experience,
    • (iv) 56 points, for three years of Canadian work experience,
    • (v) 63 points, for four years of Canadian work experience, and
    • (vi) 70 points, for five or more years of Canadian work experience.
Points for experience in high-wage occupation

(8) If a foreign national has at least one year of Canadian work experience in one or more high-wage occupations, they are to be assigned points as follows:

  • (a) 70 points, if one year of that experience was in one or more occupations in respect of which the high-wage occupation level, as it appears on the list of high-wage occupations at the time an invitation is issued, is the level of 2 times the median wage;
  • (b) 40 points, if one year of that experience was in one or more occupations in respect of which the high-wage occupation level, as it appears on the list of high-wage occupations at the time an invitation is issued, is the level of 1.5 times the median wage or a combination of that level and the level referred to in paragraph (a); or
  • (c) 20 points, if one year of that experience was in one or more occupations in respect of which the high-wage occupation level, as it appears on the list of high-wage occupations at the time an invitation is issued, is the level of 1.3 times the median wage or a combination of that level and the levels referred to in paragraphs (a) and (b).
Simultaneous work experience

(9) Despite subsection (5), simultaneous periods of work experience are to be evaluated as distinct periods of work experience for the purpose of determining which of paragraphs (8)(a) to (c) is applicable.

Points for qualifying offer of employment

15.2 (1) If a foreign national has a qualifying offer of employment in a high-wage occupation, they are to be assigned points as follows:

  • (a) 100 points, if the qualifying offer of employment is in an occupation in respect of which the high-wage occupation level, as it appears on the list of high-wage occupations at the time an invitation is issued, is the level of 2 times the median wage;
  • (b) 70 points, if the qualifying offer of employment is in an occupation in respect of which the high-wage occupation level, as it appears on the list of high-wage occupations at the time an invitation is issued, is the level of 1.5 times the median wage; or
  • (c) 50 points, if the qualifying offer of employment is in an occupation in respect of which the high-wage occupation level, as it appears on the list of high-wage occupations at the time an invitation is issued, is the level of 1.3 times the median wage.
Qualifying offer of employment

(2) Subject to subsections (4) and (5), a qualifying offer of employment is one of the following offers of employment for full-time work in Canada that has a continuous duration of at least one year from the day on which a permanent resident visa is issued to the foreign national:

  • (a) an offer of employment in an occupation listed in unit group 31100, 31101 or 31102 of the National Occupational Classification, if, during that employment, the foreign national is to provide publicly funded medical services;
  • (b) an offer of employment in an occupation listed in TEER Category 0, 1, 2 or 3 of the National Occupational Classification that is made to the foreign national by a single employer, other than an embassy, high commission or consulate in Canada or an employer referred to in subparagraph 200(3)(h)(ii) or (iii) of the Regulations, if the foreign national is to be remunerated by the payment of wages or a commission;
  • (c) an offer of employment in a skilled trade occupation, as defined in subsection 87.2(1) of the Regulations, that is made to the foreign national by up to two employers, neither of which is an embassy, high commission or consulate in Canada or an employer referred to in subparagraph 200(3)(h)(ii) or (iii) of the Regulations, if the foreign national is to be remunerated by the payment of wages or a commission.
Full-time work

(3) For the purposes of subsection (2), full-time work consists of at least 30 hours of work per week.

Genuineness of offer

(4) An offer is a qualifying offer of employment only if an officer has determined, on the basis of any information provided on the officer’s request by the foreign national or by the employer making the offer of employment and any other relevant information, that the offer is genuine having regard to the following factors:

  • (a) the employer is actively engaged in the business in respect of which the offer is made;
  • (b) the offer is consistent with the reasonable employment needs of the employer;
  • (c) the terms of the offer are terms that the employer is reasonably able to fulfill;
  • (d) the offer is for employment that the foreign national is qualified for, able to perform and is likely to accept and carry out, as indicated by their past work experience or education related to the lead statement and main duties of the occupation as set out in the occupational descriptions of the National Occupational Classification; and
  • (e) the past compliance of the employer with the federal or provincial laws that regulate the employment or recruitment of employees, including foreign nationals, in the province in which it is intended that the foreign national will work.
Loss of offer

(5) If the offer of employment is revoked, the offer is no longer a qualifying offer of employment.

5 Subsection 22(4) of the Instructions is replaced by the following:

Clarification — Canadian work experience

(4) Subsections 15.1(2) to (6) apply in respect of Canadian work experience referred to in subsection (1), with any necessary modifications.

6 Subsection 24(3) of the Instructions is replaced by the following:

Clarification— Canadian work experience

(3) Subsections 15.1(2) to (6) apply in respect of Canadian work experience referred to in subsection (1), with any necessary modifications.

7 Section 29 of the Instructions is repealed.

8 The heading before section 33 and sections 33 and 34 of the Instructions are repealed.

Taking Effect

9 These Instructions take effect on November 3, 2026, but if they are given after that day, they take effect on the day on which they are given.
