Forms Available to File Online

Alert Type info

Alert: We now offer online filing of Form I-485, Application to Register Permanent Residence or Adjust Status, for certain applicants and their representatives.

When you file Form I-485, you must also submit Form I-693, Report of Immigration Medical Examination and Vaccination Record. A civil surgeon is responsible for providing you with a completed Form I-693 that is signed and placed in a sealed envelope. If you file Form I-485 online, you must open the sealed envelope from the civil surgeon and upload the completed Form I-693 with your application package. You must keep the original Form I-693 and the envelope from the civil surgeon until we make a final decision on your Form I-485. We may review the original Form I-693 and envelope during an interview or ask you to submit the original Form I-693 and envelope as evidence to support your application.

If you file Form I-485 by mail, there are no changes to the Form I-693 submission process. You must submit Form I-693 with your Form I-485 application package, in the original sealed envelope provided by the civil surgeon.

This page lists the USCIS forms you can file online. Visit the File Online page to learn about the benefits of filing a form using our secure online system. Start by creating a free USCIS online account.

If you are immigrating to the United States as a lawful permanent resident, you must pay the USCIS Immigrant Fee online, unless you are exempted from the fee.

If you are filing Form I-131A, Application for Carrier Documentation, you must pay the filing fee for this form online.

There are 2 options for submitting forms using a USCIS online account:

  • Fill out a form through a guided online workflow; or
  • Upload a completed PDF of the form.

The available submission options depend on the form type and filing category. Both submission options give you access to the same USCIS online account case management features.

There is a discount when you file certain forms online. You can find information about filing fees for specific forms on our Fee Schedule page.

Form I-485, Application to Register Permanent Residence or Adjust Status

Filing a standalone Form I-485 

You or your attorney or accredited representative can file Form I-485 based on the following categories as a standalone form:

  • Employment-Based (Underlying Form I-140 already filed)
  • Family-Based (Underlying Form I-130 already filed)
  • VAWA Self-Petitioner (Underlying Form I-360 already filed)

Filing Form I-485 concurrently with an underlying visa petition (Form I-140 or Form I-130)

Attorneys or accredited representatives can file Form I-485 concurrently with an underlying visa petition filed for you by a petitioner.

Individuals who are filing a Form I-140 as a self-petitioner can also concurrently file Form I-140 and Form I-485.

If an attorney or accredited representative is not submitting a filing on your behalf, and you are not a Form I-140 self-petitioner, you may not concurrently file Form I-485 with an underlying visa petition. If you are ineligible to concurrently file Form I-485 with a visa petition, the Form I-130 or Form I-140 petitioner must first submit their petition through their USCIS online account.  After the visa petitioner receives their visa petition acceptance notice, and you meet all eligibility criteria to file Form I-485, you may submit your Form I-485 application through your USCIS online account.

You can now submit the following forms with Form I-485 when you file online using PDF upload:

  • Form G-28, Notice of Entry of Appearance as Attorney or Accredited Representative (if you have  an attorney or accredited representative or if you are filing as an attorney or accredited representative)
  • Form I-130, Petition for Alien Relative if being filed by an attorney, or accredited representative
  • Form I-131, Application for Travel Documents, Parole Documents, and Arrival/Departure Records
  • Form I-140, Immigrant Petition for Alien Workers if being filed by an attorney, accredited representative, or self-petitioner
  • Form I-485 Supplement A, Adjustment of Status Under Section 245(i)
  • Form I-485 Supplement J, Confirmation of Valid Job Offer or Request for Job Portability Under INA Section 204(j)
  • Form I-693, Report of Immigration Medical Examination and Vaccination Record
  • Form I-765, Application for Employment Authorization
  • Form I-864, Affidavit of Support Under Section 213A of the INA

If you file Form I-485 online and need to submit Form I-693 or a partial Form I-693 (such as the Vaccination Record), you must open the sealed envelope from the civil surgeon and upload the completed and signed Form I-693 with your application package. You must keep the original Form I-693 and the envelope from the civil surgeon until we make a final decision on your Form I-485. We may review the original Form I-693 and envelope during an interview or ask you to submit the original Form I-693 and envelope as evidence to support your Form I-485.

Form I-765, Application for Employment Authorization

You can file online for these categories:

  • (a)(12) – Temporary Protected Status granted;
  • (c)(8) – Application for employment authorization based on pending Form I-589, Application for Asylum or for Withholding of Removal, and applicants for pending asylum under the ABC Settlement Agreement;
  • (c)(9) - Certain family-based and employment-based applicants pending adjustment of status under Section 245 of the Immigration and Nationality Act;
  • (c)(11) – Paroled for urgent humanitarian or significant public benefit purposes; or
  • (c)(19) – Certain pending TPS applicants whom USCIS has determined are prima facie eligible for TPS and who may then receive an EAD as a “temporary treatment benefit” under 8 C.F.R. 244.10(a).

Note: If you are filing Form I-765 for a (c)(9) Pending Adjustment of Status category that is fee exempt, do not submit your application through the PDF filing option at this time. If you file Form I-765 for the (c)(9) category, you must pay a fee or submit a fee waiver request. If you are fee-exempt and proceed with paying the fee and submitting your application through the PDF filing option, we will not issue you a refund. If you are filing under a fee-exempt category, you should mail a paper Form I-765 to the address on the Direct Filing Addresses for Form I-765, Application for Employment Authorization webpage to receive the fee exemption.

File Online

Form I-907, Request for Premium Processing Service

Form I-907 can be filed concurrently with:

  • Form I-129 if being filed by a petitioner, attorney, or accredited representative; or
  • Form I-140 if being filed by a self-petitioner, petitioner, or attorney or accredited representative

You can file Form I-907 if the underlying form is already pending (with a receipt number that begins with IOE) and was filed either by mail or online:

  • Petitioners with a pending Form I-140, Immigrant Petition for Alien Worker.
  • Petitioners with a pending Form I-129, Petition for a Nonimmigrant Worker, for:
    • E petitions (all categories);
    • H-1B petitions (including H-1B2, and H-1B3);
    • H-2B petitions (cap-exempt only);
    • H-3 petitions;
    • L-1A and L-1B petitions;
    • LZ (Blanket L) petitions;
    • O petitions (all categories);
    • P petitions (all categories);
    • Q petitions (all categories);
    • R-1 petitions; and
    • TN petitions (all categories).
  • Certain F-1 students who already have a pending Form I-765, Application for Employment Authorization, or are filing together with Form I-765 under 1 of the following categories:
    • (c)(3)(A) – Pre-Completion OPT;
    • (c)(3)(B) – Post-Completion OPT; or
    • (c)(3)(C) – 24-month Extension of OPT for STEM students.
  • Applicants with a pending Form I-539, Application to Extend/Change Nonimmigrant Status, requesting a change of status to F-1, F-2, M-1, M-2, J-1, or J-2 status.
